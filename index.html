Research Mission

My long-term goal is to understand how supermassive black holes formed, grew, ignited into quasars during the early universe. I am especially interested in applying spectral analaysis of JWST data and combining obtained contraints with numerical modeling of early SMBH enviorments.

Featured Research Projects

ALMA CO Absorption in Active Galaxies

Archival ALMA study of carbon monoxide absorption against bright AGN continua. This project constrains molecular gas properties through spectral extraction, continuum normalization, Gaussian fitting, opacity measurements, and column-density estimates.

Tungsten-182 Isotopic Anomalies and Mantle Dynamics
