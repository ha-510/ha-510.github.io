My current research is a faint CO absorption-line census of nearby (z < 0.03), active, early galaxies using archival ALMA observations. I am building from this foundation toward graduate research on high-redshift quasars, AGN fueling and feedback, and the overall gas environments of supermassive black holes.

Research mission
