Research Projects

Observational astronomy, absorption-line spectroscopy, and computational modeling.

My work has developed along two complementary tracks: real astronomical data from ALMA and numerical simulations built to test physical hypotheses. The common thread is using technical tools to move from raw data or model output toward a physical interpretation.

Current and completed research

ALMA CO Absorption in Active Galaxies

Brigham Young University · Advisor: Dr. Benjamin Boizelle · Summer 2025–Present

Archival ALMA study of faint carbon monoxide absorption lines against bright radio cores in active galaxies. The project combines CASA imaging, spectral extraction, continuum normalization, Gaussian line fitting, opacity estimates, and molecular column-density calculations. This work is continuing as my senior honors thesis.

Tungsten-182 Isotopic Anomalies and Mantle Dynamics

Rutgers University · Advisors: Prof. Katherine Bermingham and Prof. Charles-Édouard Boukaré · Jun 2024–May 2025

Computational geodynamics project investigating the origin of tungsten-182 isotopic anomalies in mantle-derived rocks. I modified Fortran source code, ran simulations on Graham and Cedar, analyzed outputs with Python, and presented the results through McNair research settings.
